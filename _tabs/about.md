I am a software engineer by profession and a curious builder by nature. I read extensively... mostly history, law, true events, autobiographies, and books that help me understand society at a deeper level. I write blogs and long-form posts in a raw, direct style, often questioning systems and ideas rather than accepting them blindly.

I am an admirer of poetry, especially Sri Sri gaaru, whose words have shaped my thinking and helped me through different phases of life.

Outside work, I am deeply into motorcycles. I repair my own bike, understand engines and mechanical systems in detail, and create content around motorcycle maintenance and practical mechanics. I ride long distances every year, exploring places, people, and perspectives beyond comfort zones.

Music is another serious commitment in my life. I practice Carnatic flute with discipline, treating it as meditation through breath and focus.

I value rational thinking over rigid ideology, and I am preparing to enter the legal field to contribute meaningfully to justice. I believe in learning by doing... building, riding, reading, writing, and constantly questioning myself.
